How to add a playlist

  1. Build the playlist in Spotify and turn Mix on.
  2. Share it and copy the link, then paste it below.
  3. Hit generate, copy the block, and paste it into the RIDES array in ride.html.
  4. Fill in cadence, zone and cue for each segment. Track durations are in the comments so you can pick your offsets.
